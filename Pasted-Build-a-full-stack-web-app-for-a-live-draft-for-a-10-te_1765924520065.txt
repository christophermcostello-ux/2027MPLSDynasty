Build a full-stack web app for a live draft for a 10-team dynasty baseball league with commissioner controls, real-time updates, player filtering/sorting, OTL (Outside The League) drafting, and tradable picks/tokens.

League Setup & Totals (Hard Rules)

Teams: 10 teams.

Keepers: There will be exactly 100 saved players across the league going into the draft (league-wide keeper total = 100). Individual teams may have more or fewer keepers due to trades.

Draft order: Fixed order every round (NOT reversing snake):

Every round goes Team 1 → Team 10

Team 1 is always first; Team 10 always last.

Draft structure:

Main Draft (Major League / ML): 16 rounds × 10 teams = 160 picks total. Every ML pick is a 4-year contract.

Minor Draft (MiL): 10 rounds × 10 teams = 100 picks total. Every MiL pick is a 2-year contract.

The MiL draft happens after the ML draft completes.

Draft completion rule: Use Option A — the draft runs through all scheduled pick slots (ML 160 picks + MiL 100 picks), regardless of roster sizes. Teams may temporarily exceed 26 and handle cuts later; do not block picks due to roster size.

OTL (Outside The League) System (ML ONLY)

The league does not draft from all MLB teams. Players are split into:

In-League pool (normal draftable players)

OTL pool (outside the league)

The app must provide four player browsing tabs:

Batters (in-league)

Pitchers (in-league)

OTL Batters

OTL Pitchers

OTL pick rules (Main Draft only):

Each team starts with 1 OTL token (tradable).

A team may use its OTL token on any of its ML picks, but must explicitly declare “Use OTL token” during that pick.

When used, the token is consumed for that team.

OTL picks are NOT allowed in the MiL draft.

Total OTL picks in the ML draft are capped by a league setting OTL_PICK_CAP = 10 (default 10; commissioner can change).

OTL players can only be drafted from OTL tabs; non-OTL players can only be drafted from non-OTL tabs.

The OTL “Draft” button must be disabled unless:

It is the current pick owner’s turn (or commissioner override), AND

“Use OTL token” is toggled for that pick, AND

The team owns at least 1 unused OTL token, AND

The league OTL cap has not been exceeded, AND

The current phase is ML.

Trading (must support)

Trading is commissioner-controlled and recorded in an audit log.
Tradable assets:

Draft picks (any future pick slot in ML or MiL; reassign pick owner)

OTL tokens (transfer token ownership between teams)
Also support pre-draft trades affecting keepers:

Commissioner tool to move a keeper player from one team to another or remove a keeper (reflecting “player traded for pick” scenarios)

Keepers totals league-wide should validate to 100 after setup (warn if not)

Pick ownership rule:

Each pick slot has an owner pickOwnerTeamId. The “team on the clock” is the owner of the current pick slot, not necessarily the slot’s original team.
